Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — name, email, phone number, and booking details — when you register, log in, or book a service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process bookings, manage your account, send status notifications, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing (never in plain text). All database queries are parameterized to prevent injection attacks, and admin functions are protected by role-based access control.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use secure session cookies to keep you logged in, and browser local storage to remember your light/dark theme preference. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

5. Your Rights

You may update or delete your profile information at any time from your dashboard, or contact support for further assistance.
